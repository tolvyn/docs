# Emails from TOLVYN

TOLVYN sends email from three addresses, each for one kind of mail, and a reply always reaches a person. No automated email
from TOLVYN comes from a person's own address.

| Email | From | A reply goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Your account: email verification, password reset link, someone tried to sign up with your address, invitations, account deleted, access ended | no-reply@tolvyn.io | support@tolvyn.io |
| Billing: receipts, plan ended, billing notices | no-reply@tolvyn.io | billing@tolvyn.io |
| Security: your password was changed, your password was reset | no-reply@tolvyn.io | security@tolvyn.io |
| Spend alerts: budget thresholds, cost anomalies, model changes, attribution conflicts, price notices | alerts@tolvyn.io | support@tolvyn.io |
| The weekly digest, for owners who turned it on | digest@tolvyn.io | support@tolvyn.io |

Questions about using TOLVYN go to support@tolvyn.io; about plans and buying more, to sales@tolvyn.io. If an automated email
says it is from TOLVYN and comes from any other address, forward it to security@tolvyn.io.
